Pressure definition

  • First innings: overs 18–20; overs 16–17 also included when at least three wickets have fallen.
  • Chase: required rate ≥9 runs/over; or 36 or fewer balls remain with required rate ≥7.5; or 60 or fewer balls remain with at least five wickets down.
This is a reproducible proxy based on the supplied match situation fields. It does not capture every factor that makes a delivery difficult.

Scoring approach

Batters: 50% strike-rate improvement, 25% dismissal-rate improvement and 25% boundary-rate improvement.

Bowlers: 40% economy improvement, 35% wicket-rate change and 25% dot-ball change.

Small samples are discounted using pressure balls ÷ (pressure balls + 60). Minimum exposure: 120 pressure balls, 12 pressure matches and 180 total role balls.
